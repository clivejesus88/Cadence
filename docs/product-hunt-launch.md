## 5. The story / why it exists

Students don't fail to focus because they lack timers. They fail because every tool is either a bare countdown clock or a bloated productivity suite that wants a login, a subscription, and your soul before minute one.

Cadence started with a simple question: *what if a focus app felt like a quiet room instead of another dashboard?*

So we built one that:

- **Works before you sign up.** Open it, start a session. Accounts are for syncing, not for entry.
- **Works offline.** Your tasks and sessions live on your device first. The network is a sync layer, not a dependency.
- **Respects attention.** No streak-shaming, no confetti spam, no infinite feed. Just a timer, your tasks, and gentle evidence that you're improving.
- **Reminds you like a person, not a bot.** Real push notifications at sensible local times — a 4pm nudge and a 6pm recap — not a wall of pings.

It's built for the student who studies on a bus, in a library with bad Wi-Fi, and on a phone that's always one tap away from distraction.

## 6. Feature list (benefit-first)

**Plan**
- Tasks with subjects and estimated focus blocks (pomodoros).
- Planner grouped into Overdue / Today / Tomorrow / Later.
- Resume a session you had to stop — progress isn't lost.

**Focus**
- Clean focus timer with break auto-start.
- **7 free soft soundscapes** including real public-domain classical pieces (Satie *Gymnopédie*, Beethoven *Moonlight Sonata*, Pachelbel *Canon in D*) plus original cinematic piano and strings. Ambient rain, ocean, forest, and noise also included.
- **Focus shield** that nudges you back when you switch tabs or apps mid-session — and **stays quiet when your screen simply turns off**.

**Protect**
- **Distraction plan**: name what to avoid by schedule, app, or website.
- Strict mode for sessions you don't want to talk yourself out of.

**Progress**
- Streaks, levels, badges, and a consistency grid.
- Insights with charts, a heatmap, and month-long patterns.
- Daily focus goal you set yourself.

**Belong**
- Optional accounts with private cloud sync (Row-Level Security).
- **Guest mode** — full local experience, no email required.
- Real Web Push reminders, timezone auto-detected, installable to the home screen.
- Privacy Policy + Terms, data stays yours.

## 7. Audience

**Primary:** university and high-school students (16–24) who study on phones and laptops and want structure without friction.

**Secondary:**
- Lifelong learners and self-taught developers.
- Anyone who wants a Pomodoro app that isn't a game.
- Privacy-minded users who don't want an account to use a timer.

**Anti-persona (be explicit):** teams, project managers, and people looking for OS-level app blocking. Cadence is a personal study rhythm tool, not enterprise productivity software.

## 15. Likely questions & answers (prep)

**"How is this different from Pomofocus / Forest / Notion?"**

> Cadence is offline-first and guest-first: it works before you sign up and without a connection. It adds planning, resume, insights, and real push reminders without turning studying into a game or a dashboard.

**"Does it block apps?"**

> No — a web app can't block other apps at the OS level, and we won't pretend otherwise. Cadence gives you a distraction plan and a focus shield that nudges you back when you leave a session. It's honest about what it can do.

**"Is it really free?"**

> The core app is free forever: tasks, timer, breaks, core sounds, streaks, insights, guest mode, reminders. Pro is optional and funds the infrastructure.

**"Why does iOS push need an install?"**

> Apple only allows Web Push for home-screen web apps on iOS 16.4+. That's an Apple restriction. On Android and desktop it works in the browser.

**"Where is my data?"**

> On your device first (IndexedDB). If you create an account, it syncs to your own private rows in Supabase, protected by row-level security. We don't sell data.

**"Are the classical tracks real recordings?"**

> They're procedurally generated performances of public-domain compositions plus original pieces written for Cadence. We don't bundle copyrighted recordings.

**"What's next?"**

> Better insights, more soundscapes, and quality-of-life fixes from launch feedback. Tell us what's missing.
